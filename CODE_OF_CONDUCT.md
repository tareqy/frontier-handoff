# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Frontier Handoff depends on precise disagreement. Contributors may challenge model claims, methods, scores, provenance, and project decisions. They must do so without harassment or personal attack.

Expected behavior includes:

- criticizing claims and evidence rather than a contributor's identity or motives;
- declaring conflicts of interest and material provider affiliations;
- preserving good-faith disagreement and negative results;
- correcting errors transparently;
- respecting privacy, consent, confidentiality, and publication rights;
- helping newcomers understand the evidence standard.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or personal data, evidence tampering, impersonation, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where someone represents the project. Research criticism, including blunt criticism, is welcome when it remains relevant and evidence-focused.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports or identifying evidence in a public issue. Use the repository host's private reporting channel or contact a maintainer privately. If no private channel has been configured, open a minimal public issue requesting private contact without describing the incident.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports, limit access to people who need the information, avoid conflicts of interest, and document proportional action. Possible responses include a warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban. Knowingly false reports may also be addressed, but an unsubstantiated good-faith report is not a violation.

For accidental credential, private-data, or proprietary-material exposure, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) immediately.
